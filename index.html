הארנק · HaArnak

Shipped 2026 —

A personal finance app for Israeli banking. It scrapes my bank and two card issuers nightly, stores only raw rows, and derives every figure in a pure-function ledger on the client — so a categorisation, an event tag or a rent schedule can never be clobbered by the next scrape. About three years of history, ~1,770 transactions.

  • Card bills double-count by construction. The bank shows one lump debit; the issuer shows the itemised purchases. Itemised scraping only became complete in Aug 2025, so 25 earlier months had the bill removed with nothing to replace it — ₪187,193 of history invisible. Fixed by reconciling itemised rows plus the shortfall against what was actually billed, exact for all 25 months, and it self-disables as coverage improves.
  • The card silently started billing in foreign currency. Dollars were being summed into shekel totals. The original/charged ratio recovers the rate the issuer used — 3.5907 against 3.592 derived by hand — and the rescaled cycle reconciles to the bank's own figure within two agorot.
  • Rent is declared, not inferred. The previous approach matched a payment by its exact amount and spread it forward, which silently broke the moment the rent changed. Now the obligation is a versioned monthly cost and payments are matched to it, so cadence — quarterly, annual, rotating between flatmates — cannot move a monthly figure.
  • An assistant with read-only tools. Eight scoped tools over the ledger, answers returned as typed blocks (figure, calculation, chart) rather than markdown, so a chart carries its query and the client re-runs it locally. The system prompt and schema stay server-side.

משקל · Mishkal

Shipped 2026 —

Weight, waist, steps, training and macros — built because every calorie calculator asks you to self-select an activity level from a dropdown, which is a guess people get wrong in a consistent direction.

  • Apple Health has no web API. The only route without a native app is a Shortcuts automation posting the day's step count to an endpoint. It authenticates with a scoped ingest key whose entire blast radius is one step count for one user — deliberately not a long-lived user token, which would grant read and write over everything.
  • The energy model is decomposed, not declared. Basal metabolism, plus walking measured from real steps, plus training from a declared weekly frequency — kept apart so walking is never counted twice, which is exactly what applying a single lifestyle multiplier on top of measured steps does.
  • Calibrated against a published equation rather than a fitted constant, after the first version quietly told me a stated 1 kg/week goal would deliver 0.42. The floor that caused it is now surfaced instead of silently clamping.
  • Every date is an Israel-local day. Storing a date-only value as a UTC instant had already put 1,750 rows a day early in the other app; here the local-day derivation lives in one module and nothing else is allowed to compute a day.

No demo account on this one — it holds a single real user's body data, so there is nothing to hand out. Happy to walk through it live.

Synthetic speech attribution — IEEE Signal Processing Cup 2022

Team SIPL · published Mar 2022 · Zenodo

Given a recording, identify which synthetic speech generator produced it — attribution rather than detection. Signal processing features combined with deep networks in an ensemble, with augmentation for robustness to noise: 92.2% accuracy on clean samples and 78.4% on noisy ones.
